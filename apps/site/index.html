Marketing Autopilot

A tool that reads what your product does, suggests what to say, drafts posts and short videos, and schedules them to your own social accounts. Nothing is posted until you approve it.

What it does

  • Reads your product's website, code and notes to understand what it does.
  • Drafts posts, swipe posts and short videos in your voice.
  • Schedules approved posts to TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Threads, X, LinkedIn and others through Upload-Post.
  • Shows what worked: views, taps on your tracking links, and signups.

What it never does

  • Post anything you haven't approved.
  • Log in to your accounts for you. You connect them on each platform's own page.
  • Post on Reddit, Hacker News, Product Hunt or Discord. It drafts; you post.
