Privacy policy

Last updated: [date]

[Company name] runs Marketing Autopilot. This page explains what we keep, why, and who helps us process it.

What we collect

  • Sign-in: your GitHub username, name, email address and avatar.
  • Your product: the website, code, notes, screenshots and recordings you give us, and the product profile, posts and videos made from them.
  • Connected accounts: which social accounts you connected (handle and status). We never see or store your social media passwords.
  • Keys you paste: stored encrypted and never shown again in full.
  • Results: views, likes, comments and link taps on your posts, and signup counts from tracking links. These are totals, not information about individual people.
  • Spending: what each paid step cost, so we can keep you inside your limit.

Why

Only to run the service for you: understanding your product, drafting and scheduling posts, and showing results. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Temporary files are removed after 7 days, and database backups are kept for 14 days.

Your choices

You can delete everything at any time; see Delete your data. You can also email [contact email] for a copy of your data, a correction or deletion.

Security

Keys are encrypted, access is limited to invited GitHub accounts, and every post needs your approval in the app.
